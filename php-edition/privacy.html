Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026  ·  Applies to growvantacrm.online and the Growvanta CRM application

We care about your data, and we would rather be plain about it than hide behind twelve pages of legal language. This policy says what we collect, why we collect it, who else can see it, and how to make us delete it.

The short version.

We collect only what the CRM needs to work. We do not sell your data, we do not sell your customers' data, and we do not use it to advertise to anyone. There are no tracking or analytics scripts in the application. Your data is kept separate from every other company's. You can export it at any time, and you can ask us to delete it — see section 10.

1. Who we are

Growvanta CRM ("Growvanta", "we", "us") provides customer relationship management software for property sales, at growvantacrm.online.

You can reach us on +91 99109 74785 or at alert@growvantacrm.online. If you have a privacy question, that email address reaches a person, not a queue.

2. Two different kinds of data

This distinction matters, so it comes before everything else.

Your data

Information about you and your company as our customer — your name, your work email, your phone number, your company's details, and what you do inside the application. We decide how this is handled, so we are responsible for it.

Your customers' data

The leads you put into the CRM — the names, phone numbers and enquiries of people interested in your properties. This data is yours. You decide what to collect, who in your team sees it, how long to keep it and when to delete it. We only store and process it on your instructions, as the software you are using.

Practically, this means: if a buyer asks you to delete their details, you can do that yourself inside the CRM. If they contact us directly, we will point them to you, because it is not our data to remove on our own initiative.

4. Why we collect it

  • To run the service — you cannot have a CRM without storing the leads in it.
  • To keep your account secure — sign-in records let us and you spot access that should not have happened.
  • To answer "who did that?" — the activity log exists because sales teams need to settle exactly this question.
  • To send the emails you asked for — new-lead alerts, follow-up reminders and the daily summary go to your own staff.
  • To support you — when you report a problem, we may need to look at the relevant records to find it.
  • To meet legal obligations — invoicing and tax records, where the law requires them.

That is the complete list. We do not build advertising profiles, we do not enrich your data from outside sources, and we do not train anything on your leads.

5. Cookies

The application sets exactly two cookies, and both are necessary for it to work. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies.

CookieWhat it doesLife
gv_session Keeps you signed in. It cannot be read by JavaScript, and it is only sent over HTTPS. 12 hours, or 30 days if you chose "remember me"

Because neither cookie tracks you, there is no consent banner to click through.

One thing worth naming: these public pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees the IP address of anyone reading them. The application itself, once you sign in, loads nothing from Google.

6. Facebook and Instagram lead ads

If you choose to connect a Facebook page, you authorise Growvanta on your own Facebook account. We then receive:

  • the list of pages you administer, so you can pick which ones to connect;
  • an access token for the pages you picked;
  • the lead forms on those pages, and the leads submitted through them.

We ask for nothing beyond that. We do not read your posts, your messages, your friends, your ad spend or your audiences. Page access tokens are encrypted before they are stored and are never displayed back to you.

You can disconnect a page from the Integrations screen at any time, or revoke Growvanta's access from Facebook directly under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations. Disconnecting stops new leads arriving; leads already delivered stay in your CRM until you delete them.

8. How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit. The whole site is HTTPS. There is no unencrypted way in.
  • Passwords are never stored. We store a bcrypt hash, which cannot be reversed into your password. If you lose it, we can reset it — we cannot tell you what it was.
  • Integration secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, with a key held outside the database. A stolen database copy does not yield working Facebook tokens.
  • Session tokens are stored hashed, for the same reason.
  • Companies are separated at the query level. Every request is bound to one company; a lead belonging to another company is not merely hidden from the screen, it is not returned by the database at all.
  • Inside your company, people see only their scope — their own leads, their team's, their region's, or everything, according to the role you give them.
  • Everything significant is logged with the person and the time.

No system is beyond compromise, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you what happened, what was affected and what we are doing — promptly, and without waiting for you to ask.

9. How long we keep it

  • Lead data — for as long as your account is active. You control it; delete any of it whenever you like.
  • Account details — for as long as your account is active, then up to 90 days, so an account closed by mistake can be restored.
  • Sign-in and activity logs — up to 12 months.
  • Invoices and tax records — as long as Indian tax law requires.
  • Trial accounts that never became customers — deleted within 90 days of the trial ending.

10. Deleting your data

You can have your data deleted, and you do not have to give a reason.

Deleting part of it yourself

Any lead, contact or document can be deleted from inside the CRM by anyone with the right permission. That takes effect immediately.

Deleting all of it

Email alert@growvantacrm.online with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email address on your account, or call +91 99109 74785.

  • We confirm within 2 working days that we have received it.
  • We complete deletion within 30 days.
  • We tell you when it is done.
  • Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, within 90 days.
  • We keep only what law requires us to keep, such as invoices — nothing else.

Export before you delete. Deletion cannot be undone. You can export your leads to Excel at any time from inside the CRM, and we will help you do it if you ask.

If you connected Facebook

To remove the data associated with a Facebook connection, either disconnect the page from the Integrations screen, or remove Growvanta from Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations on Facebook. Both remove our access and our stored token for that page. To also delete the leads already delivered to your CRM, use the deletion request above.

Write to alert@growvantacrm.online. We reply within 30 days, and we do not charge for any of this.

12. Children

Growvanta CRM is a business tool. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child's data has reached us, tell us and we will remove it.
