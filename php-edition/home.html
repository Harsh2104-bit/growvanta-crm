What this is

A CRM that only does property.

General-purpose CRMs are built around a "deal" and a "contact". Property sales does not work that way. The buyer is the lead, the deal is a booking against a specific unit in a specific project, and the same person may enquire three times about three towers.

Growvanta is built around that shape. Projects, configurations, site visits, booking stages and re-enquiries are part of the model, not custom fields bolted onto something meant for software sales.

The problem it solves

Leads arrive in five places at once — Facebook's inbox, a WhatsApp group, an email, a portal, a spreadsheet. Nobody is sure who is calling whom. By the time a lead is assigned, the buyer has spoken to three other builders.

What changes

Every source feeds one list. Assignment happens automatically the moment a lead lands. The salesperson gets an email straight away, and again before the follow-up is due. Nothing sits unclaimed.

What your manager sees

Who is sitting on leads, which project is converting, how many site visits happened this week, and which follow-ups were missed — without asking anyone for a report.

What you can do with it

Built for a sales floor, not a demo.

Capture leads from everywhere

Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads connect with one button. Website forms embed anywhere. Bulk lists import from Excel or CSV. Anything else can post to the API.

Assign without arguing

Rules decide who gets what — by project, by source, by team, or simply in turn. The lead is owned within seconds of arriving, and the handover is recorded.

Never miss a follow-up

Schedule the next call on the lead itself. The salesperson gets an email before it is due, and a summary every morning of that day's follow-ups and site visits.

Keep your inventory straight

Projects hold their own configurations, price bands, amenities and status. Every lead is attributed to a project, so you always know which tower the enquiry was for.

Decide who sees what

Roles control what a person can do; hierarchy controls whose leads they can see — their own, their team's, their region's or everyone's. A junior cannot export the whole database.

See what is actually happening

Source performance, stage-by-stage conversion, salesperson activity, site visits and bookings. Filter it, then export it to Excel when somebody wants it in a meeting.

Talk to buyers from one place

Email and WhatsApp templates, sent against the lead and logged on its timeline. A channel only goes live once a test message has actually arrived — so nothing quietly fails.

Catch duplicates before they embarrass you

The same buyer enquiring twice is flagged, not duplicated. Two salespeople never call the same person about the same project on the same afternoon.

Know who changed what

Every status change, reassignment and export is recorded with the person and the time. When a lead goes cold, you can see exactly what happened to it.

Questions people ask

Straight answers.

What is Growvanta CRM?

Growvanta CRM is a customer relationship management system built only for property sales. It collects enquiries from Facebook and Instagram lead ads, website forms, spreadsheet imports and its API, assigns each one to a salesperson automatically, and tracks it through follow-ups and site visits to a booking.

It differs from a general CRM in its model: the buyer is the lead, and a deal is a booking against a unit in a specific project, rather than an abstract "opportunity".

Is there a free trial, and do I need a credit card?

Yes to the trial, no to the card. Growvanta CRM is free for 30 days with every feature unlocked, and no card details are asked for to start or during the trial. Nothing charges itself at the end, because there is no card on file to charge.

What does it cost after the trial?

Pricing is agreed with you in writing before any invoice is raised, based on how many salespeople need a login. Call +91 99109 74785 for a figure for your team size. There is no surprise pricing and no automatic renewal charge.

Can it capture leads from Facebook and Instagram ads automatically?

Yes. You connect your own Facebook page with one button, choose which pages belong in the CRM, and every lead form on them starts delivering — including forms you publish later. You never handle a page ID or an access token.

Can I import the leads I already have in Excel?

Yes. Excel and CSV files import in bulk, with duplicate detection so the same buyer is flagged rather than entered twice. Most teams import their existing list and start calling the same afternoon.

Does it work for a channel partner selling for several builders?

Yes. Each builder's projects, teams and leads are kept separate, with their own assignment rules and their own reporting, while you see across all of them.

Who in my team can see which leads?

You decide, on two axes. A role controls what a person can do; a visibility scope controls whose leads they can see — their own, their team's, their team and everyone below, their region's, or the whole company. A junior salesperson cannot export the full database.

Can I export my data and leave?

Yes, at any time, without asking permission. Your leads export to Excel from inside the CRM, during the trial or long after. Your data belongs to you, and ending your account does not hold it hostage.

Is my data safe?

Every company's data is separated at the database query level, not merely hidden on screen. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and integration secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The whole site runs over HTTPS, and every status change, reassignment and export is logged with the person and the time. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work on a mobile phone?

Yes. Growvanta CRM runs in the phone's browser with no app to install, which matters for a sales team that is out on site visits rather than at a desk.

How long does setup take?

Usually the same working day. We create your company's space, your admin login and your team's logins; you import your leads and connect your Facebook page yourself from the Integrations screen.
