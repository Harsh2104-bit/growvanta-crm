WhatsApp & Email

Automation that tells you the truth

Most tools show a switch. Switch on, assume it works, find out weeks later that nothing ever sent. Here a channel reports one of three states, and only one of them means messages are going out.

Red

Off

The channel is switched off. Nothing sends, and the screen says so plainly.

Amber

On, but not finished

Switched on and still incomplete — a missing token, an unapproved template, no successful test yet. The remaining items are listed by name.

Green

Live

Configured, tested, and actually delivering. Green is earned by a test that landed — never by the switch alone.

Nothing fails silently. When a message is skipped, the delivery log records the reason in words — channel off, template not approved, no phone number on the lead — instead of leaving a gap you would have to notice yourself.
